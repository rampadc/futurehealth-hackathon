# Physical Health Companion — Business brief

**Idea:** Shame-free offline pet kit that programmes issue so people stay on the plan after week 4.  
**Solution lives in:** `../ideation/` · **Narrative:** `../pitches/PITCH_BRIEF_Physical-Health-Companion.md`  
**Status:** Concept. Hardware cost not proven. Sell to programmes, not the App Store.

---

## The line

Abu Dhabi already pays for weight, ageing, and school activity programmes. The leak is people who quit after week 4. That leak is a kit budget, not another app.

**Payback rule:** A kit is worth buying only if it lifts 16-week completion by about **10–15 points** versus a cheap pedometer and a reminder. If it doesn’t, don’t manufacture it.

Weight Management already showed outcomes when people stay (~6,500 people in year one; ~14% average weight loss among participants). The commercial question is completion, not whether lifestyle care works.

---

## Who pays

**Start here:** DoH / ADPHC Weight Management. Second door: ADEK / school health. Consumer retail is a fallback, not the plan.

| Who | Why they act now | What they buy |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight Management (DoH / ADPHC)** | Reimbursement needs people who finish. Sahatna loses them. | Kit + clinician report per participant |
| **ADEK / school health** | They are already buying student wearables and wellbeing software | Class kits + activity reporting |
| **Healthy Horizons (PureHealth / FDF)** | Seniors who will not live in a phone app | Caregiver-capable kits |
| **Thiqa / Daman (adjacent)** | Engagement-linked spend is wasted on dropouts | Proof someone stayed in the programme |
| **Consumer** | Only after a programme result exists | Retail pet, health mode optional |

**Why now:** ADEK wearable / wellbeing RFQ (closes 9 Nov 2026). ADPHC already ran a school wearable pilot (2025). About 1 in 5 UAE children meet activity guidelines. Sahatna + Ultrahuman covers phone users and still misses kids, seniors, and people who quit after a bad week.

## Competitors

| They sell | Players | Why we still win |
|---|---|---|
| A phone pet | Finch, Koi, Amaru, Habitica | No clinic contract, no offline kit, weak clinical evidence trail |
| A pet fed by Apple Health | Nularo, Habitvs | iPhone users only |
| The national app + a wearable | Sahatna, Pura, Ultrahuman, Apple Watch | Wins engaged adults. Loses lapses, kids, seniors |
| School step trackers | Whoever wins the ADEK / ADPHC wearables buys | Measures steps. Doesn’t handle shame or recovery |

**Positioning:** Finch proved people like a pet. Sahatna is the system of record. We are the pocket object programmes hand out when the phone loses the person.

## Battlecards

### Finch / Koi / Amaru

- **They:** Polished self-care pets. Often free. Gentle missed days.
- **Gap:** Phone required. Not something ADPHC can issue with a signed care plan.
- **Say:** “Same psychology, different buyer. We are a programme kit.”
- **Don’t say:** “Apps don’t change behaviour.” Finch’s users say otherwise.

### Sahatna + Ultrahuman

- **They:** Official health app, wearables, biological age. Distribution we will not beat.
- **Gap:** People mute the app. Seniors and children are a bad fit.
- **Say:** “Sahatna stays the record. We are the adjunct for drop-off.”
- **Don’t say:** “Sahatna isn’t enough” as an attack.

### School wearables (ADEK / ADPHC shape)

- **They:** Bands, dashboards, MVPA reports. Buyers already know this shape.
- **Gap:** Week-1 steps, then loss. Ranking and shame are common.
- **Say:** “Judge us on week 12, against a pedometer, not on day-1 step counts.”

### Habitica-style games

- **They:** Points, parties, penalties when you miss.
- **Gap:** Punishment is a bad fit for weight and children’s programmes.
- **Say:** “A health programme cannot ship ‘your pet dies if you relapse.’”

## Offer and plan

1. Kit + reader + signed programme bundle for one beachhead (weight **or** schools).  
2. Clinician report that separates sensed activity from self-report.  
3. Usability with 20–30 people, then a study versus a pedometer.  
4. Only then a bulk price for the next procurement cycle.

**12 months:** one programme letter of intent → usability pass → ethics path for the comparator study → ten willingness-to-pay conversations.

## Objections

**“Finch is free.”**  
Finch is not a clinic-issued kit with an evidence trail. Different budget.

**“Sahatna already tracks this.”**  
For people who keep the app open. Budget the people who don’t.

**“Hardware is a bad hackathon bet.”**  
Agree. Demo the behaviour rules in software. Hardware only if the pedometer comparison wins.

**“Adults won’t use a pet.”**  
Test it. If they won’t, sell to schools or use a plain adult face. Don’t assume.

**“This becomes a medical device.”**  
Keep claims to habit support inside a clinician’s plan. No diagnosis.

**“Unit economics are fantasy.”**  
Until a bulk buyer and a real bill of materials, yes. Don’t pitch a consumer brand.

**“The ADEK RFQ closes this week.”**  
Use it as proof of budget. Bid the next cycle with a design partner, not a rushed non-compliant offer.
