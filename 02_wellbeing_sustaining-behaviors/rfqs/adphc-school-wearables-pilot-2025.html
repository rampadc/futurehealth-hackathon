Rfq-Wearable Technology in Schools Pilot Campaign - UAE Tender

The ADPHC ABU DHABI PUBLIC HEALTH CENTER, a Government sector organization in UAE, has announced a new tender for Rfq-Wearable Technology in Schools Pilot Campaign. This tender is published on UAETenders under AET Ref No: 117504843 and is categorized as a Tender. Interested and eligible suppliers are invited to participate by reviewing the tender documents and submitting their bids before the deadline on 2025-04-18.
