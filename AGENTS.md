# Product and scenario copy

Write product copy as brief, formal instructions, actions, or statuses. Present the hackathon scenario as the intended product experience. Keep demo and implementation explanations in presenter or engineering documents, not ordinary product screens.

Before using completed-status words such as “stopped”, “submitted”, “sent”, or “accepted”, identify the case event that supports them. Do not claim an offline action reached another person. Check every screen and translation that uses the same state. Preserve accurate safety and identity meaning.
