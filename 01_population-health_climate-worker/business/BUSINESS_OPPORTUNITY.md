# Heat-Safe Crew — Business brief

**Idea:** Crew-level heat stop + claimable heat-delay log (no worker ranking).  
**Solution lives in:** `../ideation/` · **Narrative:** `../pitches/PITCH_BRIEF_Heat-Safe-Crew.md`  
**Status:** Pre-revenue. Numbers are directional.

---

## The line

The UAE already bought the rulebook. Nobody sells a stop that crews will take **and** that the client can pay for. That gap is the budget.

**Payback:** ~**USD 25k** Year 1 on a 250-worker site. Repaid if you avoid **one** serious heat incident **or** turn **two** disputed delay days into a logged claim.

---

## Who pays

**Start here:** one client (signs the side letter) + one principal contractor (runs the sites). Do not sell wearables to workers.

| Who | Why they act now | What they buy |
|---|---|---|
| **Client / developer** | Delay, LDs, ESG — heat cost is invisible | Heat allowance + audit log |
| **Principal contractor** | Fixed price + ADOSH heat programme, no clean stop signal | Site kit + summer software |
| **Subcontractor** | Unpaid stops eat margin | Paid rest that claims against the log |
| **ADPHC / DMT** | Need ADOSH in construction without a surveillance state | Pilot / programme adjacency |
| **Future Health / DoH** | Need a pilot path after the challenge | Intro to a site sponsor |

**Why now:** MoHRE midday ban and fines (AED 5k/worker, cap AED 50k). ADOSH heat programme at ≥35 °C. DMT is buying ADOSH implementation in construction (RFQ closes 9 Nov 2026). EGA already spends on heat wearables — budget exists; construction still doesn’t get paid rest.

## Competitors

| They sell | Players | Why we still win |
|---|---|---|
| A band on each worker | Kenzen (EGA), SlateSafety, 36Zero | Stigma on output-paid crews. No “who pays for the rest.” |
| A weather station | Scarlet TWL, site WBGT meters | Shows the air is hot. Doesn’t stop the crew or create a claim. |
| A big HSE platform | HeatGuard, QHSE suites | Wide, vitals-heavy, weak heat-delay contract. |
| Posters + midday ban | Current default | Cheap. Mornings still hurt and stops stay unpaid. |

**Positioning:** Wearables ask “is this body hot?” Meters ask “is this air hot?” We ask “can this crew keep working, who pays for the stop, and can we prove it without naming anyone?”

## Battlecards

### Kenzen (and EGA-style wearables)

- **They:** Per-worker core-temp alerts. Real UAE trials.
- **Gap:** Individual targeting. Rest often still unpaid. Workers can refuse the band.
- **Say:** “Wearables don’t fix unpaid stop and peer pressure. Use them later, opt-in, for medical programmes.”
- **Don’t say:** “Wearables don’t work.” EGA’s summers show they can, inside a high-control plant.

### SlateSafety (band + beacon)

- **They:** Rugged wearable + site heat/noise beacon. Sold into the GCC.
- **Gap:** Often ACGIH-style thresholds, not ADOSH TWL + a heat-delay clause.
- **Say:** “Their sensor can feed us. We own the crew rule, the languages, and the claim.”

### Scarlet TWL station

- **They:** Live TWL/WBGT on a pole. Right index for the Gulf.
- **Gap:** Environment only. One point on site. No contract layer.
- **Say:** “Partner. We are the operating layer on top of a meter like theirs.”

### HeatGuard / 36Zero

- **They:** GCC heat + HSE dashboards, sometimes labour analytics.
- **Gap:** Looks like surveillance. Heat-delay contracting is thin.
- **Say:** “We are the specialist heat control plane, not another HSE suite.”

### Compliance only

- **They:** Midday ban and a paper heat plan. Nearly free.
- **Gap:** No morning microclimate, no paid rest, no live proof.
- **Say:** “That is the control arm. We have to beat it on incidents and claimable hours or we don’t deserve the budget.”

## Objections

**“Kenzen already does this.”**  
They sell body monitoring to plants with medical teams. Construction’s problem is unpaid rest and face-saving. Different budget: schedule insurance.

**“We already do the midday ban.”**  
That’s the floor. Morning heat and unpaid stops still cost money. Compare us to compliance-only, not to a blank site.

**“The client won’t pay for heat delay.”**  
One side letter. Claims only against the log. If they refuse, the product shrinks to compliance software — say that.

**“Crews will ignore it.”**  
They ignore stops that cut pay. Paid rest is the product. Measure adherence.

**“False alarms kill productivity.”**  
Track false reds. Presenteeism is the expensive miss. One real incident costs more than a year of software.

**“TAM is made up.”**  
Lead with the USD 25k site, not national GDP. Diligence is three HSE interviews and one summer.

**“This is only a hackathon.”**  
Correct. The ask is a design partner and a pilot, not a growth round.
