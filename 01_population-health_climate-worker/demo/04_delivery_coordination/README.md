# 04. Move the delivery with the supplier's agreement

The held civil work affects a booked truck delivery of aggregate: sand, gravel or crushed stone. Yard heat and receiving capacity make the original time difficult. Noura Al Nadir, Sandline's logistics coordinator, can request a later slot, but only the supplier can accept it.

Noura drafts and approves a request for a 3:30 p.m. delivery, then sends it to the supplier's dispatcher. At that point the booking is still awaiting a reply. The supplier subsequently accepts the change, so the revised slot can be shown as confirmed. This sequence makes clear who proposed the change and when an outside party agreed to it.

Confirmation of a slot does not mean the truck has arrived or the material has been delivered. Any supplier charge, decision about whether Dune or Sandline pays or delay to a deadline agreed with the client remains for Amal Al Nadir, the project manager, and the relevant parties to review. The revised booking also does not clear the civil crew to restart exposed work.
