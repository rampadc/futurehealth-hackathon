# 04. Move the material delivery with the supplier's agreement

The civil task remains held, and four qualified workers are doing light preparation in the covered workshop. A material truck was originally booked for 11:30 a.m., but yard heat and receiving capacity make that time difficult. Noura Al Nadir, Sandline's logistics coordinator, can ask for a later slot. Only the supplier controls whether its dispatcher can accept the change. The delivery book needs to show that distinction, so the yard does not prepare for a truck on the strength of a proposal alone.

## Follow the booking

1. Put the original 11:30 a.m. booking beside the held civil work and the yard constraint. A change to the work plan does not move a truck automatically. Noura identifies 3:30 p.m. as the requested slot and gives yard heat and receiving capacity as the reasons.
2. At 8:20 a.m., Noura drafts the request. The assistant can help present the reason and proposed time, but the draft commits nobody. At 8:21 a.m., Noura approves it from Sandline's side. This authorises a request; it is still not an agreement with the supplier.
3. At 8:22 a.m., the request is recorded as sent to the supplier dispatcher. The booking should remain pending at that point. A sent message establishes transmission, not that the supplier has a truck available or has agreed to the slot.
4. The dispatcher accepts at 8:23 a.m. Only then is the revised 3:30 p.m. booking confirmed. The sequence lets the yard and project team see exactly when an outside party took responsibility for the new time.

Confirmation concerns a future arrival. There is no record that the truck reached the gate, was unloaded or supplied the civil task. A supplier charge may arise, but no amount or allocation is agreed here. Amal Al Nadir, the project manager, owns any Dune–Sandline cost review and any discussion of a client milestone through the relevant contract process. Neither a confirmed slot nor schedule pressure clears exposed work while the heat hold remains.

## Where the case stands

The request passed through draft, approval and transmission, and the supplier accepted the later booking. Physical delivery, charges and any deadline treatment remain open. Rashid Al Wadi's safety decision about the civil task is separate. The connected [handover](../10_handover_and_evidence/README.md) should carry the confirmed booking without describing a completed delivery.
