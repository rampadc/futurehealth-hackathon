# 05. Keep new starters on suitable work

Noor Al Nadir and Adil Al Wadi have recently joined Dune's civil crew. Both are on the second day of gradually getting used to work in the heat, and Huda Al Nadir, their foreman, has them on supervised light work. Heavy exposed trench work needs people, so a proposed move may seem useful on the roster. For new starters, an assignment can also carry pressure to accept work that conflicts with a restriction. The mistake needs to be caught before either worker begins the task.

## Follow the roster check

1. Start with the existing roster. Noor and Adil have a supervised light civil assignment under Huda. The team needs to see the operational restriction relevant to the proposed task, without disclosing a diagnosis or full personnel record. There is no reason to turn a suitability check into a general discussion of their health.
2. At 8:00 a.m., Huda attempts to assign both workers to heavy work in the exposed civil area. The assignment check compares that task with their acclimatisation status and rejects the move. The assistant can give Huda a plain explanation and help her review a feasible roster; it cannot waive the restriction or silently mark them fully acclimatised.
3. At 8:01 a.m., Huda checks their original light-work assignment. It remains in place. The rejected proposal has not left the two people unassigned, put them in the exposed trench or claimed that they performed work they never did.

The result is a real staffing limit for the heavy task. Amal Al Nadir, Sandline's project manager, may need to consider it when planning the work sequence or reviewing a later programme change. It is not a payroll event and does not change Dune's subcontract. Huda can explain the restriction in operational terms without labelling the new workers as unreliable. Sandline's heat programme also relies on hydration, acclimatisation and self-pacing when interpreting environmental controls; even a favourable area reading would not erase an individual assignment restriction.

## Where the case stands

The attempted heavy-work move is rejected and Noor and Adil remain on supervised light work. The team still needs a staffing plan for heavy civil work when current site conditions allow it. This record does not establish a heat hold, a restart, wages paid or a commercial adjustment.
