# Example interaction — Sensor Failure

**Operator:** Civil TWL monitor t1 failed. Electrical monitor t2 reads 195 W/m²; can we use that?

**Assistant:** No. The electrical-zone measurement cannot clear the civil zone. Civil exposed tasks are held pending a fresh local assessment (SOP-04; p_stale).

**Operator:** Check the old civil reading after six minutes.

**Assistant:** It is stale and the sensor is failed. Clearance remains blocked; HSE must restore or replace local measurement.

**Operator:** The crew wants to know why the beacon cannot clear them while the electrical area looks safe.

**Assistant:** P03 can explain that t2 measures a different work front and civil conditions remain unknown. C01 follows the existing protective and manual-verification instruction now. P01 can review any actual delay later; the cost question cannot postpone protection.
