# 06. Say when local conditions are unknown

The heat monitor serving the civil work area fails. A monitor in the electrical area still has a current reading, and the civil monitor has an older reading. Neither tells the crew what conditions are like at the civil work front now.

Maya Al Qamar, the safety officer, first tries to use the electrical reading to clear civil work. That attempt is rejected because it comes from another area. After time passes, the old civil reading is also rejected because the instrument has failed and the reading is stale. The old value remains available as history, but it is not presented as a current all-clear.

Maya and Huda Al Nadir, the civil foreman, follow the manual verification and protective procedure while Rashid Al Wadi, the superintendent, arranges a local assessment. A fresh, verified local measurement and the required human review are still needed. Amal Al Nadir can review any resulting delay later; pressure to stay on schedule does not make the missing measurement reliable.
