# 10. Hand over the open case clearly

The incoming supervisor needs one account of the same civil heat case followed through [the hold](../02_heat_alert_response/README.md), [workshop reassignment](../03_constrained_reassignment/README.md) and [material delivery change](../04_delivery_coordination/README.md). The work front has been stopped, people have moved, and a supplier has agreed to a later truck booking. None of that means the exposed civil task is ready to restart. Rashid Al Wadi, the superintendent, must hand over the recorded facts and the checks still missing, while Amal Al Nadir, the project manager, receives a separate chronology for the unresolved cost question.

## Follow the case record

1. Begin with the case and alert references rather than a fresh summary invented from memory. Maya Al Qamar recorded the local heat hold at 8:01 a.m.; Huda Al Nadir acknowledged it, confirmed work stopped and recorded eleven people in recovery at separate times. The handover can cite each entry. It should not turn the first alert into proof that every later action happened at once.
2. Record the narrower schedule recovery step. Four qualified civil workers moved to light preparation in the six-seat workshop after the whole-crew move failed. Their old task and recovery places were released, leaving seven of the original recovery group in the shelter. The civil trench remains held even though workshop work is active.
3. Record Noura Al Nadir's delivery request and the supplier's acceptance. The material truck has a confirmed 3:30 p.m. booking, but it has not arrived or unloaded. The new booking is an external agreement about time, not a completed delivery or a safety clearance.
4. At 8:30 a.m., Rashid records the handover with the heat alert and delivery references. He highlights the two open safety items: heavy civil work remains held, and the next supervisor must confirm a fresh local reading and permit before any restart review. The handover is a transfer of responsibility, not a closure entry.

Amal receives the stop, recovery, four-person alternative-work and booking chronology for the Dune–Sandline commercial review. Dune's ordinary-shift Heat Rest Schedule is relevant to what Huda can tell covered workers, but the site record does not verify payroll. Nor does it establish supplier charges, a subcontract reimbursement or a payable client claim. Those decisions need the parties' own records and authority.

## Where the case stands

The incoming supervisor can see what happened, what was merely proposed and what remains open. The delivery slot is confirmed; physical delivery is not. Exposed civil work is still held pending current evidence and a human restart decision. Amal owns the unresolved cross-party cost allocation.
