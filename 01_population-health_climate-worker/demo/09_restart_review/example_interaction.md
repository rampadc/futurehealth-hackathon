# Example interaction — Restart Review

**Operator:** Civil TWL improved to 175 W/m². Restart the held trench automatically?

**Assistant:** No. P02 must review fresh local TWL, monitor health, people, water/rest, permit and midday restriction (SOP-08; p_restart).

**Operator:** P02 completes the review at 08:02 and authorises restart at 08:03.

**Assistant:** Restart recorded. At the later 08:10 attempt the reading is stale, so work returns to held and new clearance is blocked (p_stale).

**Operator:** The programme is slipping. Does the earlier approval let us keep working or establish a delay claim?

**Assistant:** No. P04 checks the permit and P02 authorises against current conditions at the action time. The stale local evidence blocks further clearance; P01 reviews any actual programme cost separately.
