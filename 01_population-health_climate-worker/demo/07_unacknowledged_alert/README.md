# 07. Follow up when an alert has no acknowledgement

The civil-area heat monitor has triggered a hold, but the register has no acknowledgement from Huda Al Nadir, the civil foreman. A busy radio channel or a missed entry could explain the gap. It does not prove that the crew ignored the instruction, and it does not prove that work stopped.

When the acknowledgement window passes, the open alert is escalated to Rashid Al Wadi, the superintendent. Rashid receives the original task and alert details and must verify the crew's actual state. The hold stays in place while acknowledgement, confirmation that work stopped and recovery remain unconfirmed. Escalation gives the response a clear owner; it is not a substitute for the missing evidence.

Amal Al Nadir, the project manager, may later review the time lost or a disputed interval, but only after the event times are reconciled. A commercial account of the delay must follow what actually happened on site.
