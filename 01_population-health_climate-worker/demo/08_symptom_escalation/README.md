# 08. Respond to a worker's symptoms

Bilal Al Safa reports dizziness while the current civil-area heat reading is outside the site's automatic hold band. His symptoms still need attention. The monitor describes conditions in the area; it cannot decide whether Bilal is well enough to keep working.

Huda Al Nadir, the civil foreman, records the report with only the detail needed to act and removes Bilal from the active task. Salim Al Wadi, the trained first aider, records an assessment and referral decision through the site's first-aid route. These are separate events: the initial report is not a diagnosis, and a responder's action does not establish medical clearance or completed follow-up.

The response follows the person rather than waiting for an environmental threshold. Bilal is not given a performance label in the shared account. Any question about wages or absence belongs in Dune's employer process, outside the crew event record.
