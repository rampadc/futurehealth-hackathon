# 03. Find work that fits the people and the space

The exposed civil task is still on hold, and Huda Al Nadir has sent the crew to recover. Moving everyone into the covered workshop looks like a way to keep work moving. It cannot work: two people are already there, leaving four places, and only some civil workers have the preparation skill needed for the workshop task. Recovery also comes before any new assignment.

The whole-crew move is rejected with those reasons shown. After recovery and a suitability check, Huda assigns Rana Al Nadir, Yusuf Al Wadi, Dana Al Qamar and Bilal Al Safa to the light preparation task. Their previous assignment and shelter occupancy are cleared when the new assignment takes effect. The rest of the crew remains accounted for; the workshop does not exceed its capacity.

Exposed civil work remains held. Amal Al Nadir, the project manager, receives a record of the four people doing alternative work and the time the remaining workers spent waiting for a later cost review. That record does not by itself decide what Dune or Sandline owes the other.
