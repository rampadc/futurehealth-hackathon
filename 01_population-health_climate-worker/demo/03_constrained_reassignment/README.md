# 03. Find alternative work that actually fits

The civil heat hold from the [previous step](../02_heat_alert_response/README.md) is still in force. Huda Al Nadir has confirmed that exposed work stopped and recorded eleven crew members in the recovery shelter. The civil roster has fourteen people in all, including Huda and two new starters already assigned to supervised light work. Under output pressure, moving everyone to the covered workshop may look like a quick way to keep the shift productive. It fails two practical checks: the six-seat room already has two welfare attendants preparing supplies, and the full civil group does not have the qualification for the light pipe-marker task. Recovery must also come before a new assignment.

## Follow the assignment

1. Begin with the held task and the shelter record. Huda tries a whole-crew workshop move at 8:10 a.m. The proposed list includes all fourteen civil staff, while only four workshop places are open. The assignment check rejects it for both capacity and qualification. It does not turn the hold into permission to work somewhere else.
2. Review the narrower option. Rana Al Nadir, Yusuf Al Wadi, Dana Al Qamar and Bilal Al Safa have the preparation skill. Huda checks that their recovery and suitability allow them to take the light task. The assistant can make the capacity and skill reasons visible, but the decision to assign people belongs to Huda through the site process.
3. At 8:11 a.m. Huda records those four on workshop preparation. Their old task reservations and shelter occupancy are released as the new assignment takes effect. The two welfare attendants plus four civil workers fill the six places exactly, with no one shown in two locations at once.
4. Seven of the eleven workers originally recorded in recovery remain in the shelter. Huda and the two supervised-light new starters are part of the fourteen-person civil roster, but were not among those eleven recovery entries. Keeping these counts separate matters: a small useful workshop crew does not mean the whole civil crew has been reassigned or that the trench has reopened.

The four-person work record and the remaining recovery or standby time can help Amal Al Nadir, Sandline's project manager, review Dune's lost output and any later cost question. Alternative work is not an automatic reimbursement offset, and the assignment record does not establish anyone's pay. Dune's employer process and the parties' contract process handle those separate questions.

## Where the case stands

The whole-crew proposal was rejected with reasons. Four qualified workers have a recorded workshop assignment without overcrowding or double booking; seven of the original recovery group remain there. Heavy exposed civil work stays held. The next connected step is [Noura's material delivery request](../04_delivery_coordination/README.md), which also cannot clear the heat hold.
