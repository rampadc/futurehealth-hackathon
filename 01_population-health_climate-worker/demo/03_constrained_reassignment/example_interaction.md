**Assistant:** No. Two of six seats are already occupied; the whole-crew proposal exceeds capacity and includes people without the required preparation qualification. After recovery and suitability are confirmed, four qualified civil workers C05–C08 can do lightweight pipe-marker checks there (SOP-05). The rest plan is not displaced.

**Operator:** C01 approves that subset. Reserve their workshop seats.

**Assistant:** C05–C08 released from trench/recovery reservations and assigned to workshop at 08:11. The other seven workers recorded in shelter A remain there. C13 and C14 retain their supervised light assignment; C01 remains the foreman. The trench hold remains active. P01 can review actual alternative work and remaining standby later; the assignment does not settle reimbursement or payroll.
