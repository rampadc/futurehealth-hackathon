# Heat programme and illustrative site SOP

**Published requirements:** ADOSH-SF CoP 11, version 4, pp 6–10 calls for heat risk assessment, a site heat stress programme, acclimatisation, communication of conditions and symptoms, TWL assessment processes, potable water, rest and cooling, first aid access, and records. The source also assigns employer and employee responsibilities. The MoHRE summer midday break applies here 12:30–15:00, 15 June–15 September; this fictional project assumes no exemption.

**Guidance and reported practice:** ADOSH-SF Technical Guideline pp 3–4 describes TWL and its hydrated, acclimatised, self-paced assumptions and measurement inputs. Its p 22 inspection checklist and p 23 contractor coordination flag inform site review. This demo accepts a sensor's synthetic TWL reading and does not derive TWL from weather or reproduce the p 5 table. EGA and Emaar references in `sources.json` are contextual reported practice, not complete site procedures.

**Fictional Sandline policy:** Hold exposed *heavy* work in the affected zone at TWL ≤150 W/m². Mark a reading stale after 5 minutes. If a hold lacks crew acknowledgment after 2 minutes, escalate to the superintendent. Do not automatically restart when TWL improves. Restricted workers remain on supervised light tasks during acclimatisation. Treat symptoms as a separate first aid trigger regardless of environmental readings. These numbers and workflow gates are invented for the demonstration.

**Fictional Sandline/Dune [Heat Rest Schedule](HEAT_REST_SCHEDULE.md):** Dune's off-site contract manager Mina Rahman confirms protected paid rest for required heat recovery in ordinary rostered shift time for the civil crew. C01 can explain this approved scope, but the register's stop and recovery entries are not payroll proof. If coverage is absent or unverified, state the uncertainty and refer the pay question to Dune; stop, water and cooling proceed immediately. This employer commitment is distinct from any subcontract reimbursement or client heat-delay allowance, neither of which is agreed in this demo. P01 Amal Al Nadir owns the unresolved cross-party commercial review. This paragraph is a fictional scenario assumption, not a legal or contractual assertion about a real site.

**Human flow:** HSE reviews local readings and alerts the foreman; foreman confirms stop and personnel accountability; welfare team offers water and cooled recovery; first aider manages symptomatic people and referral. Permit coordinator and supervisor check fresh readings, staffing, qualifications, welfare, permits and midday restriction before a restart decision. Logistics coordinates delivery changes with supplier dispatcher. Record source, timestamp, actor, acknowledgments, reasons and unresolved items. Only minimal symptom information appears in the shared log; clinical details stay in the first aid record.

## Numbered illustrative workflow

1. **SOP-01 — Before shift:** HSE and foremen review the heat risk assessment, acclimatisation roster, water and cooling capacity, permits and forecast. A forecast only shapes timing; a fresh local measurement is required for workfront controls. Record the source and review time. [CoP 11 pp 6–8; fictional workflow]
2. **SOP-02 — Measure and communicate:** Record local TWL with zone, sensor, units, measurement time and quality; communicate the applicable controls to each affected employer and crew. Confirm that the monitor is serviceable. TWL assumptions include hydration, acclimatisation and self-pacing. [CoP 11 pp 6–8; TG pp 3–4]
3. **SOP-03 — Hold and account:** For this fictional site, a valid local TWL ≤150 W/m² triggers a heavy exposed-work hold in that zone. The existing beacon alerts locally; HSE issues a case-linked instruction under P02's site authority; the subcontractor foreman acknowledges, stops the task, accounts for workers and directs water/rest/cooling. These actions have separate timestamps. C01 communicates the verified Heat Rest Schedule scope to the crew and directs individual pay questions to Dune's employer channel; no cross-party cost agreement is needed before protection. No other zone's reading can clear this one. [fictional site policy; CoP 11 programme duties]
4. **SOP-04 — Escalate:** If the foreman has not acknowledged after two minutes, notify the superintendent and keep the hold open. If a device fails or local reading is more than five minutes old, block clearance and obtain a fresh local assessment. [fictional site policy]
5. **SOP-05 — Reassign within constraints:** Check individual acclimatisation, task qualification, supervision, capacity and actual occupancy. Release prior task and recovery-place reservations before reserving a new work location. A civil workshop subset may prepare lightweight pipe markers; this does not clear the civil trench hold. [fictional site policy]
6. **SOP-06 — Respond to symptoms:** Any reported symptoms prompt immediate contact with the trained first aider and removal from the task regardless of TWL. The first aider determines assessment/referral; record the event and restrict shared data to minimum necessary information. No software diagnosis. [CoP 11 pp 6–9]
7. **SOP-07 — Coordinate delivery:** Logistics drafts a revised slot, approves the commercial request, sends it to the supplier dispatcher and marks it confirmed only on supplier acceptance. The client milestone is tracked separately and cannot override safety controls. [fictional site policy]
8. **SOP-08 — Review restart:** Superintendent and permit coordinator review fresh local TWL, healthy monitor, people, water/rest, permit and the midday restriction at the time of restart. Improved TWL alone never restarts work. Record the human authorisation or unresolved blocker. [fictional site policy; CoP 11 programme duties]
9. **SOP-09 — Handover:** Cite case and alert IDs, acknowledgment, stop, recovery, reassignment, supplier status and outstanding checks so the next shift can verify current conditions. Hand P01 the separate unresolved cost-allocation question, with source records and no inferred entitlement. Do not imply a closed case when a hold remains. [fictional site policy; CoP 11 recordkeeping]
