# Challenge fit

How Heat-Safe Crew meets each "Your solution should" item of the Climate-Resilient Worker Health challenge, and where to see it. "Built" means it runs in the submitted prototype; "Partial" and "Roadmap" are stated plainly. Limits are in [assumptions and limitations](assumptions-and-limitations.md).

| # | Requirement | What we built | State | Where to see it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dynamic heat-risk assessment | Site Thermal Work Limit (TWL) per hour, in sun and shade, from ERA5 or forecast weather (`compute_heat_stress`), mapped to the ADOSH-SF zones, with withdrawal limits and the midday break. Per worker, `compute_worker_risk` recomputes TWL for their clothing, sets work/rest limits for their workload, and raises the band for acclimatisation, a report of feeling unwell, low fluid, rising strain from a consented wearable, or a lost input; red-flag symptoms are an emergency. Crew band = the highest, with the share at Amber or above. A valid local reading corrects the weather only toward caution; a faulty or stale one raises every band | Built. On 4 Aug 2025 at 08:00 the same air puts work-wear workers at Amber and coverall wearers at Red. The demo crew's clothing is synthetic; hydration and physiology inputs exist in the function, but nothing feeds them yet | Squad Monitoring and Crew & workshop (heat risk by worker); worker Now (my heat risk); presenter preset 06 |
| 2 | Clear, graded, multilingual warnings | A fixed-text stop-work instruction and worker alert with set actions. Worker screens in English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Malayalam, Bengali and Tagalog, right-to-left where needed. The replay's alert templates cover tighten, rest now, sensor fault, withdraw and emergency | Built on screen. Translations are not reviewed by native speakers; Tamil, Nepali and Pashto are listed but not translated. SMS and voice delivery are not sent | Site Stop-work; worker Alert and Now |
| 3 | Worker-first governance | Consent per data type that the worker can withdraw; a worker sees only their own plan and records; supervisors see "needs rest now" and each worker's band and action, never the reasons, answers or body data; only the worker sees why. The Planning Agent refuses ranking, discipline, attendance and diagnosis. Proposals are never applied by software | Built in the app's access rules and agent instruction. Database-level isolation (row-level security, separate service accounts) is roadmap | Worker Welcome and My data; System & trust guardrails |
| 4 | Field-ready operation | Worker check-ins and symptom reports queue offline and send once on reconnect. A failed or stale sensor never clears a hold. A high-contrast glare theme on the phone screens, glove-sized targets, a shelter kiosk with NFC badges on Android | Partial. No on-device rule table, cached forecast or SMS fallback; no live sensor adapters | Worker Offline; presenter sensor-fault preset; kiosk |
| 5 | Supervisor decision support | Heat controls with the evidence needed for clearance; protective response with owners and an overdue escalation; the crew eligibility funnel and a solver work/rest plan; a restart review with all eight checks; a handover that carries open decisions. The Planning Agent answers from the live case, cites guidance and can ask the solver "what if" questions without recording anything | Built | Supervisor web screens; Ask the Planning Agent |
| 6 | Safety and productivity measures | A one-day replay comparing no plan, a morning plan, and Heat-Safe: minutes over the work limit (280, 20, 0), work by zone, midday-break and withdrawal compliance, detection delay, faults caught, false withdrawals, work delivered and shortfall | Built for one synthetic replay day. A full-summer backtest is roadmap | [`metrics_replay_2025-08-01.md`](metrics_replay_2025-08-01.md) |

## Trust and evidence

- Every solver result carries `applied: false`. Only named people record instructions, stops, assignments and reviews; the case ledger keeps their records apart from proposals.
- System & trust lists solver runs, people's records and agent answers with the tools each used, filterable by kind.
- Agent evals: golden scenarios for the supervisor agent and a retrieval set, scored in code (`evals/README.md`). The case agent's tool gate is unit-tested; it has no live golden eval yet.
