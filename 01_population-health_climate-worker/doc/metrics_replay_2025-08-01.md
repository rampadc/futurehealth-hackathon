> **Prototype replay, not field results.** One day, one crew (steel fixers in shade, 5 h of work, acclimatised). Heat is the real ERA5 replay day with a **SYNTHETIC** site sensor: a +3 °C heat spike 08:15-09:15 and one faulty reading at 09:40. **No heat illness is simulated:** the safety measure is work beyond the ADOSH-SF work/rest limits (TG Table 1, rolling 60 minutes), judged against measured heat, not illness prevented. Generated by `tools/replay_metrics.py` (rules 2026.11.14-1).

Heat-Safe:** the same plan, the fast loop's alerts, and a re-plan at 09:00 on measured heat, counting the minutes actually worked.

## What the fast loop did (C)

- Alerts: **4** from 168 readings (withdraw 2, rest_now 1, sensor_fault 1); 28 messages (supervisor + 6 worker languages each).
- Detection delay: **0 min** from the first reading worse than the plan to the first alert (readings every 5 minutes).
- Sensor faults caught: **1**; false withdrawals: **0**.
- Minutes the fast loop cut before the re-plan: **15**; minutes the re-plan moved to other hours: **45** (re-plan from 09:00, 120 min actually worked before it).
- C's shortfall is work the monitor would not allow under the measured heat (the rolling work limit, and the 30-minute holds after a withdrawal limit is reached). It is reported to the supervisor as a shortfall, never squeezed into unsafe time: the cost of protection, made visible.

## Not measured here

Adherence, symptom reports and escalations need the worker app and check-ins (roadmap). A full-summer backtest with synthetic crews is also on the roadmap; this is one replayed day.
