# Supervisor workspace

The response desk is the entry point for the current incident and its open responsibilities. It separates ready actions for the selected role, work waiting on other people, and overdue actions with recorded deadlines. The demo still maintains one current incident; the ten scenarios are operator controls, not ten simultaneous incidents.

Open an action to inspect the incident. Hold issuance, acknowledgement, confirmed work cessation and recovery are distinct records. A completed step must not imply the next step occurred. Approvals continue to use the deterministic scenario engine and named actor.

Recovery and delivery decisions use the shared commercial planning assumptions. Worker-hours describe potential productive time over the next thirty minutes, not measured output or payroll savings. A proposed delivery slot does not replace the confirmed booking until supplier acceptance.

Site and shift connects the selected work front with its scheduled tasks, contractor and assigned people. Policies and execution history remain accessible as supporting evidence. Handover preserves unresolved responsibilities.

The presenter remains on `/presenter` in a separate tab. Only the presenter chooses scenarios or advances external events. The stakeholder workspace has no scenario library.
