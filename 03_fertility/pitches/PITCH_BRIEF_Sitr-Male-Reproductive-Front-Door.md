# Sitr (ستر) — Hackathon Pitch Brief
### Private front door for male reproductive health

**Challenge track:** Fertility → Male reproductive health (bridges reproductive potential / inherited-risk navigation)  
**Stage:** Research-backed concept only. No signed clinic partnership; no Malaffi write access; no shipped product.  
**One-liner:** Culturally trusted, privacy-first digital intake and triage for men — earlier risk signals, lower-friction assessment, safe referral — screening, not diagnosis.

## 1. Problem

Abu Dhabi is building an integrated **Future of Reproductive Health** ecosystem (prevention → genetic screening → fertility care → ART → preservation → genomics). Female and couple pathways are increasingly structured. Male reproductive health still often arrives **late** — after prolonged difficulty conceiving, after the female partner has already been assessed — under stigma that keeps men offline.

| Stakeholder | Pain |
|---|---|
| Man / couple | Stigma; late entry; fear of exposure; unclear starting point |
| Female partner | Absorbs early investigative burden alone |
| Primary care / premarital | Infectious + genetic screens exist; male lifestyle / andrology triage uneven |
| ART centres | Severe male factor presents late; sensitive-data trust load |
| DoH / ADPHC | Strategy says upstream + shared responsibility; male front-door UX still thin |

**Structural context:** Premarital screening (ADPHC) including genetic panel for Emiratis (570 genes / 840+ disorders); Emirati Genome + Malaffi as consented clinical–genomic fabric; strong ART delivery (e.g. Fakih IVF, CCAD); research MoUs on severe male infertility are long-horizon science, not a consumer MVP. Challenge brief itself calls for a trusted front door that reduces stigma, protects sensitive data, and separates screening from diagnosis.

**Insight:** the emirate invested in the reproductive **back end**. The missing product is a **front door for men** that cultural reality will actually use.

## 3. Solution

*Intended product design for a concept submission. Clinical and cultural review mandatory before any pilot.*

### What it is

Private Arabic/English digital intake and triage for men: consented history plus lifestyle / metabolic / infectious / anatomical risk signals; explainable, non-stigmatising results; routing to self-care, primary care, andrology/urology, fertility services, or urgent assessment — with red flags and human oversight explicit.

### Principles

1. Screening ≠ diagnosis — never output “you are infertile.”  
2. Privacy by design — purpose consent, minimisation, user-controlled sharing.  
3. Cultural trust — discreet brand (*Sitr*), no assumptions about family choices.  
4. Low friction — private intake, teleconsultation, at-home steps only where appropriate.  
5. Safe triage — deterministic red flags + clinician review for high-risk paths.  
6. Measurable value — completed assessments, time-to-evaluation, referral completion, trust/fairness.

### Modules

| Module | User experience | Backend |
|---|---|---|
| Private onboarding | Pseudonymous start; purpose consent | PDPL / health-data aligned controls |
| Risk interview | Structured history (metabolic, infection, meds, occupational heat, surgery, lifestyle) | Rule/model scores labelled as screening |
| Explainable results | “Signals that may warrant a conversation” + next steps | Guideline citations; no diagnostic claim |
| Care navigation | Tele-andrology / primary care; DoH-licensed centres | Human confirmation for referrals |
| Couple mode (opt-in) | Controlled share to partner / clinician | Per-field, revocable |
| Premarital bridge (later) | Education adjacent to genetic counselling | Never replaces ADPHC genetic process |
| Clinician console | Referral pack | Access audit |

### Non-goals (MVP)

Semen photo ML / at-home lab diagnosis; gene-editing or investigational spermatogenesis in consumer UX; automatic Malaffi writes without partnership; scraped clinic “success rate” rankings.

### Demo vs full product

| Near-term | Full solution |
|---|---|
| Synthetic cases + explainable triage demo | Clinically governed risk content |
| Mock booking flows | Live scheduling with 1–2 licensed partners |
| Trust metrics on simulated cohorts | Real referral completion / time-to-evaluation |
| AR/EN policy + UX | Premarital / EGP-adjacent consented pathways |

## 4. Future state

*Aspirational 3–5 years if partnerships and evidence work — not present tense.*

- Men complete a private reproductive check-in years before “we’ve been trying 12 months,” without a fertility waiting-room stigma moment.
- Primary care and premarital pathways treat male assessment as expected.
- When genetic carrier results need reproductive options counselling, the male partner enters with dignity and prepared questions — app never speaks in absolutes.
- ART centres receive earlier, consented referral packs; severe male factor identified sooner.
- DoH can study aggregated, consented male preconception patterns (distinct male/female models) without a stigma machine.
- Trust and fairness metrics published; brand *Sitr* means covered, private, respectful.

| Stakeholder | Future state |
|---|---|
| Man | Checked early, privately, knew next step without humiliation |
| Couple | Entered fertility care together |
| Clinician | Earlier structured, consented referrals |
| DoH / ADPHC | Measurable, culturally safe upstream male engagement |

**Category endgame:** default male preconception / early-risk interface for Abu Dhabi’s reproductive programme, then GCC markets with similar stigma/regulatory shape. Asset = clinical governance + cultural trust + consented routing — not a quiz funnel.

**Gates:** cultural/clinical advisory sign-off → design partner (DoH-aligned or licensed ART) → prototype eval (red-flag recall, no over-diagnosis language) → micro-pilot (completion, referral, trust) → only then any Malaffi/genomic adjacency. Fail early gates → do not position as consumer growth app.

## 5. Commercial (investor-ready facts)

**Model:** B2B2C via DoH programmes, primary care, ART centres (per assessment + navigation licence or programme subscription); optional discreet direct access with tele-andrology revenue share.  
**Not the model:** selling identified reproductive data; ads; employer packages.  
**SOM honesty:** gated by trust and partnership milestones, not vanity downloads.  
**Competition:** global fertility apps / at-home kits often culturally mismatched or diagnosis-blurry; local ART brands are strong clinically but thin pre-clinic; premarital genetics is state process — bridge, don’t replace.  
**Risks:** UX stigma backlash; medical-claim overreach; breach severity; slow enterprise sales without RFQ; genomics temptation before trust.  
**Illustrative ask:** UX + clinical protocol + prototype + ethics path for micro-pilot — not a lab. 12-month success: governed triage prototype; signed design partner; AR/EN cultural review; pilot protocol; “never diagnose” eval harness passing.

## 6. Hackathon narrative

1. **Problem:** male reproductive care enters late and under stigma.  
2. **Insight:** Abu Dhabi built the back end; men need a private front door.  
3. **Solution:** explainable screening triage + licensed navigation + privacy by design.  
4. **Demo:** culturally careful intake → risk signals (labelled screening) → referral path → red-flag escalation; refuse diagnostic overclaim.  
5. **Future state:** early, covered male engagement as routine.  
6. **Pathway ask:** clinical/cultural design partner in the reproductive ecosystem.
